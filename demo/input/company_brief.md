# Company Brief — Northwind Traders (Fictional Demo)

> Fully synthetic demo data. Northwind Traders, its products and all figures
> in this dataset are fictional and were created for demonstration purposes
> only. No real client data is used.

## Task

Prepare a market entry report for the fictional company Northwind Traders,
a specialty coffee retailer considering expansion into Germany and Austria.

## Target Audience

C-level executives: CEO, CFO, VP of Strategy, Head of International Expansion.

## Deliverable

- Format: market entry report
- Length: 5-7 pages
- Language: English

## Key Questions the Report Must Answer

1. How large is the specialty coffee market in Germany and Austria, and what is the forecast for the next five years?
2. Who are the main competitors and what does the competitive landscape look like?
3. What entry barriers exist (regulatory, cultural, infrastructural)?
4. What are the practical recommendations for market entry?

## Business Context

Northwind Traders currently operates 42 retail locations in Scandinavia.
The board is evaluating Germany and Austria as the next expansion region.
The final report will be presented to the board of directors.

## Constraints

- All figures and facts must come exclusively from the sources listed in the methodology.
- If data for a question is not available, use the label "Data not available".
- Report deadline: two weeks.
